### [Eulogy To A Hell Of A Dame](https://allpoetry.com/Eulogy-To-A-Hell-Of-A-Dame)
#### charles bukowski wr. ~1983 
*...you
wanted to explode out of  
what was holding you...*  
...  
*here's a drink  
to your bones  
that  
this dog  
still  
dreams about.*
